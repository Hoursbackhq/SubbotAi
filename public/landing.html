How it works

Claim, set aside, forget.

01

Claim your G$

Claim your daily GoodDollar income right inside SubBot. It goes to your own wallet.

02

Fill a bill jar

Make a jar for each bill, like DStv or your NEPA meter. SubBot shows how much is set aside and how much is still short.

03

It pays on time

Turn on autopay and the bill is paid on the due day, once per cycle. You get the token and the receipt.
